Lending Risk Analysis

25,000-loan book modeled on the public LendingClub schema. Measures default rate by grade, vintage, purpose, and DTI to identify where risk concentrates in a portfolio and how it shifts over time.

  • Default rate climbs from 8% (grade A) to 53% (grade G)
  • 2020-issued loans default higher across every grade — a vintage effect, not a grading failure
  • Default rate rises sharply past 30% DTI

Rental Portfolio Analytics

Cash-flow, occupancy, and expense tracking for a 4-unit rental portfolio, demonstrating unit level P&L analysis used to guide pricing and capital improvement decisions.

  • Net cash flow varies meaningfully by unit before financing costs
  • A single vacancy month has an outsized effect on annual return
  • Property tax and insurance dominate the expense mix; repairs are the most variable line
